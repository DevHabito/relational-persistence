All eight trials completed: four positive controlled (two per
runner), two positive controller-disabled, and two zero-dose shams.
Each positive intervention delivered 120 accepted HTTP work units;
both shams delivered exactly zero. No controller action or numeric
candidate index was invented for disabled or sham trials.

The monitor directly recorded queue/worker status against external
injector-end events, with a sampled 500-ms idle dwell and a maximum
150-ms permitted interval between eligible samples. **Unobserved
states between probes are not certified idle.** Monotonic-clock
subtraction is performed only within each individual host run.

The table is rounded for reading; the original binary floating-point
outputs, timestamps, full sample streams, and all source hashes are
preserved in Git. `B` means **local pre-intervention saturated
throughput**, not a certified global capacity. `I` is the accepted
injection work rate; `tau` uses the service-applied change
timestamp, not a programmed delay or response acknowledgement.
`Y` uses sampled health state independently of `B,I,tau,C`.

**Do not interpret the four tuples as a fitted or held-out predictive
test.** The experiment intentionally repeats one narrowly
controlled software protocol; source workloads, feedback mechanism
and observed ranges have insufficient independently randomized
variation to isolate how well C predicts Y. The two environments
share the same code, cloud provider and intervention mechanism.
A correlation computed retrospectively from four tuples would
not validate a universal persistence relation or show a causal effect.

## Regression and integrity controls

The original outcome was recomputed from all raw role files by
[`rp_e005_cross_runner.py`](../../experiments/rp_e005_cross_runner.py)
and an additional grouping-based sampled-recovery implementation,
[`rp_e005_idle_oracle.py`](../../experiments/rp_e005_idle_oracle.py),
independent of P02's own `replay_monitor` function. These
software cross-checks do not supply an independent physical
recovery instrument.

The new
[`test_rp_e005_original_archive.py`](../../tests/test_rp_e005_original_archive.py)
permanently checks the **archived SHA-256 index**, requires all
53 archived file hashes to match original bytes, recomputes the
entire two-runner decision from the committed source streams
and rejects a deliberately corrupted trace. Changes to
historical measurements must cause regression failure,
not a silently changed positive result.

## What remains open

This milestone upgrades repeatability evidence from one hosted
VM to two separately provisioned VM jobs. It **does not prove**
independent physical instrumentation, external transfer,
continuous absence of queued work, causal identification,
prediction by the ratio, or the correctness of a universal
physical stability law.

A valid subsequent predictive design needs genuinely
different disturbance intensities and independently measured
reaction latencies, a classical FIFO/queueing competing model,
a reasonable uncertainty and held-out sampling plan, and
unambiguous handling of zero-dose and no-controller cases.
Those requirements should be frozen in a **new** protocol;
the P01 first-run data are not prospective test targets for
a hypothesis devised after inspecting them.

No new Lean 4 theorem was needed or claimed for this
software-instrumentation replication. The repo's existing
Lean results remain separately verifiable.
