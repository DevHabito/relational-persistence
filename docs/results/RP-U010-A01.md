# RP-U010-A01 — DAYPSCI institutional source access: DOI verified, experimental files not resolved

**17 November 2026 (Brazilian local date). Result: `RP_U010_A01_METADATA_ONLY_SOURCE_FILES_NOT_RESOLVED`.**

This result is about the ability to **identify, authenticate and later inspect an external industrial dataset**. It is **not an experiment on process recovery**, and it is not evidence for or against a biological, physical or universal law of relational persistence.

## Why this study was opened

The preceding RP-U009 study attempted to relate physical railway compressor pressure to operator-reported maintenance. The first original run was invalid because the source encoded its binary switch as decimal text. A separately registered diagnostic corrected the representation, but the second, disclosed post-source analysis failed the unchanged six-hour temporal-coverage requirement. **No eligible pressure-recovery outcome** came from RP-U009.

DAYPSCI is an interesting next candidate because the authors used Siemens S7-1200/S7-1500 PLC equipment in a controlled industrial testbed and applied known sensor and solenoid-valve fault scenarios. Their 2026 article documents an event-based design and batches of processed CSV events, PROFINET PCAPNG traffic, and experiment-specific descriptions. The paper includes `ts_plc`, `delta_time_ms`, `scan_id`, `event_order`, `part_id`, `Gemma`, `Anomaly` and `Fault`. We verified these statements **from the published paper**, not from downloaded original experimental files.

This design potentially makes event ordering and disturbance introduction easier to study than MetroPT maintenance logs. It does **not** by itself show that a fault is turned off during an experimental batch or that the industrial process resumes independent functional production. The hybrid anomaly labels use both experiment configuration and controller-runtime states; we cannot automatically treat them as independent physical outcome measurements. The presence of two timestamped file types does **not** establish that PLC and PCAP clocks share a verified time base.

## Original gate outcome

**`RP_U010_A01_METADATA_ONLY_SOURCE_FILES_NOT_RESOLVED`**.

The study has positively verified **only dataset identity from DataCite**. It does not meet the registered threshold for a source-file manifest. We cannot legitimately report the number of complete fault-injection cycles, count of batches, time to restore marking, joint PLC/PCAP synchronization or a relation between injection and successful production from this source.

The next source gate is to obtain a **specific original publisher file URL or bundle manifest**, ideally with stable size/UUID/checksum, and then authenticate individual files from that official release. Until this is resolved, designing a recovery test based on example filenames or invented process outcomes would repeat the source-assumption problems seen in earlier studies.

## Explicit checks still needed before a real RP-U010-P01 trial

| Scientific requirement | Status after A01 | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Publisher dataset DOI and title | **VERIFIED** via DataCite | Distinguishes a named source from an invented citation |
| Concrete individual CSV/PCAP/README listing and original hashes | **NOT VERIFIED** | Prevents silent source substitution |
| Real event/scan chronology within original CSV | **NOT INSPECTED** | `ts_plc` and `scan_id` in the article are not empirical data |
| Fault activation **and** removal within one observed batch | **NOT VERIFIED** | Experimental configuration labels may not record a repaired condition |
| Independently recorded task completion/marking success | **NOT VERIFIED** | `part_id` or `Gemma` alone may not certify physical function |
| PLC and PCAP clock synchronization | **NOT VERIFIED** | Without an offset or synchronization proof, absolute event-order claims across files are unsafe |
| Confirmed relational/physical persistence increment over established models | **NOT TESTED** | Requires all earlier gates and genuine held-out outcomes |

The next useful methodological move is **not** a positive hypothesis test. It is an official-file manifest acquisition and timestamp/endpoint feasibility audit, followed by a separately registered and source-hashed original study only if all basic conditions are met. If the university repository remains inaccessible, request a source-file listing and checksum inventory from the dataset's authors or curator before downloading from an alternate host. Do not replace the source by an unattributed mirror.
