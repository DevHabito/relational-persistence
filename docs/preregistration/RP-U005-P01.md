# RP-U005-P01 — Structural path restoration versus observed backlog recovery

**Protocol-only freeze:** 17 November 2026. **No RP-U005 code, original result, source archive, or Lean proof exists yet at this point.** This study is synthetic and deterministic. Prior RP-U001 to RP-U004 graph-repair results are already known; no independent empirical, biological or physical claim follows from this model.

## Why this study is needed

The earlier RP-U004 experiment measured F1 (one reachable source-target path) and F2 (two internally vertex-disjoint paths) after component repair. In its finite study family, two-step F2-aware repair beat one-step greedy under shared graph information. However, F1/F2 are **structural** booleans; they do not directly measure whether a service backlog is eliminated after a fault. The program's research scope requires an independent, precisely declared operational function before asserting "functional persistence."

This is a modest, established queue/network-science distinction, not an originality claim. Prior work includes Holling (1973), resilience versus stability, DOI 10.1146/annurev.es.04.110173.000245; Hu et al. (2016), recovery after localized network attacks, DOI 10.1038/srep24522; *Recovery patterns and physics of the network* (2021), PLOS ONE, DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0245396, which explicitly measures delivered demand during network restoration; *Sequencing post-disruption concurrent restoration via a network flow approach* (2024), Transportation Research Part D 133:104234, which uses flow-based restoration; and *Enhancing Network Failure Mitigation with Performance-Aware Ranking* (USENIX NSDI 2025), which evaluates flow-completion time and throughput rather than topology alone. These sources do not imply novelty of the present arithmetic witness.

## Frozen synthetic network, source and repair

Fix four nodes V={0,1,2,3}; source 0, destination 3. Original, undirected, unit-capacity edges are (0,1), (1,3), (0,2), (2,3). There is no direct source-destination edge and no alternate path. Each two-hop source-to-sink route can serve **one unit-sized job per discrete tick** in this deliberately simplified abstract *parallel-server service network*. Transport propagation delay, packet collisions, loss, service variance, finite buffers, batching, changes in physical reference frame, and adaptive rerouting are **not represented**. One tick is a model tick, not a measured second.

At model ticks t=0,1,2, remove only edge (1,3), leaving a single available path, so structural F1=1 and F2=0; at t=3 restore exactly that edge, thereafter never change it, so F1=F2=1 for t=3..11. In this model alone, operational capacity is declared as c(t)=1+F2(t) (one or two unit service slots per tick). This is a **model bridge**, not a physically measured law or independently identified relation-to-capacity mapping.

Use horizon t=0..11 inclusive. Define exactly eight scenarios: a pre-repair input a_pre∈{0,2} applied at t=0,1,2; and a post-repair input a_post∈{0,1,2,3} applied at every t=3..11. Initial job backlog B_0=0. An arrival is an externally specified unit-sized job; demand is not inferred from the graph or the recovery outcome.

Each tick follows a fixed causal order: (1) apply the declared edge failure/repair, (2) append a(t) arrivals, (3) serve up to c(t) waiting jobs FIFO, (4) observe the **end-of-tick** queue length B_{t+1} and the completed-job count d(t). The independent functional criterion is **two consecutive end-of-tick measurements with B=0**, with both observations occurring no earlier than t=3. The recovery timestamp is the **second** such tick, not the beginning of the window. If no such pair is observed by tick11, report recovery as null/right-censored, not infinity. Record served arrivals and backlog exactly at all twelve ticks, not only summary recoveries.

No post hoc retiming, demand replacement, fitted capacity, changed service target, silently discarded unfinished jobs or selective scenario omission is allowed. Jobs may remain queued after horizon without being counted as delivered.

## Distinct independent program oracles

The primary queue implementation uses the exact nonnegative integer recurrence:

- q_0=0;
- n_t=q_t+a_t;
- d_t=min(n_t,c_t);
- q_{t+1}=n_t−d_t.

A separate **event-queue oracle** must create individual unit jobs (distinguished by arrival tick and within-tick index), enqueue them FIFO, and pop exactly min(capacity, waiting) units, without calling the recurrence's update function. After each tick compare the two full outputs (q_{t+1},d_t,c_t), not only their totals. Check both path criteria with two **independent graph algorithms**: a direct BFS reachability oracle, and exhaustive simple paths with pairwise internal-vertex disjointness; also independently check F2 by deleting each internal vertex in turn and requiring surviving reachability. Any inconsistency invalidates the computation.

Conservation is required on each scenario and prefix: cumulative arrivals = cumulative delivered + current backlog. Validate source and destination degree, active graph edge identity, unchanged postrepair topology, exact fixed arrival trace, nonnegative integer queues, service budget, horizon, duplicate scenario keys and all eight original cases.

## Registered hypotheses, controls and what we expect

**Primary structural-sufficiency challenge H1.** Compare scenarios (pre=0,post=1) and (pre=2,post=1), which have exactly the **same graph and F1/F2 trajectories, identical postrepair input, service capacities and repair actions**, but a different undisclosed pre-repair service history. Require two finite recovery timestamps and **stressed_recovery_timestamp − clean_recovery_timestamp ≥ 2 ticks**. If satisfied, report \`FINITE_TOPOLOGY_INSUFFICIENT_FOR_RECOVERY_TIME\`; otherwise \`FINITE_REGISTERED_SEPARATION_NOT_OBSERVED\`. This is a constructive finite counterexample to **sufficiency of topology-plus-postrepair-demand alone** for predicting this queue recovery time, not to every possible relational theory.

**Registered secondary challenge H2.** For (pre=2,post=2), the restored network has c=2 and a=2 from t=3 onward, while the residual backlog is positive. Require F2=1 on every t≥3 **and no registered idle recovery through tick11**. Record this as \`BALANCED_CAPACITY_DOES_NOT_DRAIN_EXISTING_BACKLOG\` if confirmed. This follows arithmetically from q+a−c=q when a=c, not a novel queue theorem.

**Negative controls** must all be reported:
- (pre=0,post=2): an empty backlog remains empty after repair even at balanced capacity.
- (pre=2,post=0): positive residual backlog **can** drain when restored service exceeds arrivals.
- (pre=0,post=3): with arrivals exceeding restored service, backlog emerges after repair even without pre-repair overload.
- (pre=2,post=3): the stressed backlog grows further after restoration.

These are anticipated from the declared queue dynamics. Stating them here avoids pretending the results were unknown or statistically surprising. The experiment's legitimate research value is **auditable separation of topological and operational observations** and careful operationalization of a cross-level measurement gap. It cannot demonstrate that the exact queue equation models brains, cancer, ecology, power grids or actual Internet traffic.

## Formal mathematical statements to check separately

In Lean 4 over ℕ, with a,c,b natural nonnegative integer values, prove:
1. balanced service: (b+a)−a=b for any backlog and arrivals;
2. empty queue with sufficient service: if a≤c, (0+a)−c=0;
3. strict decrease under capacity surplus: if b>0 and a<c, then (b+a)−c < b, assuming Nat truncated subtraction (the formula captures the recurrence as min(n,c) service);
4. a finite witness for the registered postrepair stressed and clean recurrences; only claim that finite witness if the proof is actually compiled by Lean CI.

A proof of these arithmetic facts is classical. It does **not** certify a physical capacity model, full Python program, arbitrary stochastic queues or cross-domain persistence.
