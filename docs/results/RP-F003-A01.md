## Exhaustive result

Every one of `2^15 = 32,768` labeled undirected simple graphs was
decoded. Exactly **26,704** are connected, spread over **68** sorted
degree signatures (67 with multiple labeled representatives). The
committed [result JSON](RP-F003-A01.json) preserves the complete
68-group distribution and exact integer arithmetic.

For `W = Id-L/10`, `T=20`, and the registered basis-ensemble
mean-zero **squared energy**

`Q20(G) = [tr(W^40)-1]/5`,

the exhaustive maximum between **same-degree-sequence connected
graphs** was attained by the masks below:

| Graph | Mask | Edges | Exact Q20, approximately |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| A: five-cycle with a pendant vertex | 700 | (0,3), (0,4), (0,5), (1,2), (1,4), (2,3) | 0.011653552669428385 |
| B: triangle with a three-edge tail | 760 | (0,4), (0,5), (1,2), (1,3), (1,4), (2,3) | 0.05373223666682076 |

Both have **six edges**, degree signature `(1,2,2,2,2,3)`,
and the same vertex count and fixed update gain. They are not
isomorphic: one contains a triangle and the other has only a
five-cycle.

## Independent arithmetic and controls

The primary enumeration uses **only integers**: `M=10 Id-L`,
`M^20`, and exact comparison of the numerator
`sum(M^20_ij²)-10^40`, divided by `5×10^40`.
It does not use floating-point eigensolvers, rounded comparisons or
tolerances. A separate matrix multiplication and power routine
checks `tr(M^40) = sum(M^20_ij²)` for both selected graphs.

The implementation also checks six-by-six dimensions, matrix
symmetry, row-sum conservation and adjacency/degree consistency,
invariance under a fixed reverse-vertex relabeling, the complete
graph's exact control `Q20(K6)=(2/5)^40`, and the disconnected
empty-graph exclusion. Malformed masks, mismatched degree
sequences and mutated matrices have explicit failing regression
tests. No graph is excluded because of its measured Q20.

These are checks on finite arithmetic and the implementation, not
independent physical observations. An implementation bug shared by
two computational methods remains possible.

## Exact Lean 4 scope

`DegreeMatchedWitness.lean` expresses graph A and a **relabeling**
of graph B so that their degree matches at **every labeled vertex**.
For the same explicit zero-sum state with `x[0]=1`, `x[2]=-1`
and other entries zero, their first post-update values at vertex 0
are exactly `7/10` and `3/5` respectively.

This is a precise **one-step** consequence of the established rational
`update` operator. It is separate from the Python-certified
`Q20` enumeration. The Lean file must pass the pinned Lean 4
workflow before it is described as *machine verified*.

## Interpretation, failures and novelty boundary

A sequence of vertex degrees does not capture all of the spectral
and structural information needed for this declared energy observable,
even though the graphs have the same total number of edges. This
strengthens the finite counterexample beyond RP-001's path/star pair.
It **does not** validate a topology-aware physical theory, identify
any real-world proxy, or show that degree-matched graphs generally
have large differences. Selection of the maximizing pair is
intentional and allowed by the preregistered **existence** question,
but is not an unbiased typical-case estimate.

The theoretical explanation belongs to classical spectral consensus
and graph algebra. The project contribution is the prospective
exhaustive audit with an explicit witness and falsification threshold.
A new physical discovery, the PMFF bridge and a measurable universal
persistence index remain open.
