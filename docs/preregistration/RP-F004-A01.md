# RP-F004-A01 — Frozen temporal-correlation counterexample

**Status:** prospective computational protocol; committed before implementing
or executing the RP-F004 exact enumerator. This experiment is **not blinded**:
the simple parameter values and a plausible positive contrast were chosen
from existing time-series theory. Therefore passing is a reproducibility
and model-sufficiency result, **not** a surprising discovery or an
unbiased prediction of an unknown natural phenomenon.

## Question and falsification target

Can two *specified* scalar linear systems with identical dynamics,
one-step response timing, and identical **one-time noise distributions**
nevertheless have different stationary output variances because their
noise processes have different temporal correlations?

This is a finite exact **conditional counterexample** question. It does
not demonstrate a universal physical law, an observed human/network
persistence variable, or a test against field data.

## Models, fixed before computation

1. Work in dimensionless discrete time, with
   \`x[t+1] = (1/2)*x[t] + eta[t]\`. Set \`x[0]=0\`.
2. Noise has the same fixed marginal distribution at each time:
   \`eta[t] = +1/10\` or \`-1/10\`, each with probability \`1/2\`.
   Thus \`E eta[t]=0\` and \`Var eta[t]=1/100\` in both cases.
3. Noise is a symmetric two-state Markov chain, starting with its
   uniform stationary distribution. Given the previous sign, the sign
   stays the same with probability \`p\` and flips with \`1-p\`.
   Compare ONLY:
   - W: \`p=1/2\` (temporally independent signs, lag-1 correlation 0).
   - P: \`p=3/4\` (persistent signs, lag-1 correlation 1/2).
   The noise sign lag-k correlation is \`(2p-1)^k\` for \`k>=0\`.
4. The recorded scalar proxy assignments are \`B=1\`, \`I=1/100\`
   (the noise variance), and \`tau=1\` discrete update step in both
   conditions. Therefore the purely algebraic \`C=B/(I*tau)=100\`.
   These are hypothetical dimensionless modeling proxies, **not**
   independently calibrated physical capacity, injection rate or
   service-action latency, and must not be compared with RP-E004 units.
5. The primary output \`Y(p)\` is the **stationary output variance**
   \`lim[t->infinity] E[x[t]^2]\`, if the limit exists under the
   explicit model and stationary noise initialization.
   No outcome is defined by, computed from, or estimated using \`C\`.

## Frozen decision rule

Using **exact rational arithmetic**, determine whether stationary
variances exist and are unique for W and P under the stated recurrence.
Define \`Delta = Y(3/4)-Y(1/2)\`.

- **PASS_CORRELATION_MATTERS** if both stationary values are
  uniquely determined and \`Delta >= 1/200\`.
- **NO_REGISTERED_SEPARATION** if both stationary values are
  valid and \`Delta < 1/200\`.
- **STOP_INVALID_MOMENT_MODEL** if assumptions, exact identities,
  normalization, cross-checks or uniqueness fail.

The threshold 1/200 is an **illustrative absolute output-variance
threshold**, not a fitted, clinically meaningful, or physical one.
Do not change parameters, horizons or threshold after looking
at results. No multiple-testing correction or p-value is used because
there is no random sample from nature.

## Required independent checks

1. Derive the moment update from the joint process \`(x,eta)\`:
   with \`v[t]=E[x[t]^2]\`, \`c[t]=E[x[t]*eta[t]]\`,
   \`s2=1/100\`, \`a=1/2\`, and \`r=2p-1\`,
   \`v[t+1]=a^2*v[t]+2*a*c[t]+s2\` and
   \`c[t+1]=r*(a*c[t]+s2)\`. Start with \`v[0]=c[0]=0\`.
   An exact fixed point gives the stationary candidate. Explain
   convergence and why conditional Markov behavior justifies recurrence.
2. Implement an **independent**, direct exact-rational full
   path enumeration of all \`2^12=4096\` length-12 sign histories.
   Sum \`P(history)*x[12]^2\` **without calling the moment
   recurrence**. Compare exactly to the 12-step moment recurrence,
   for both \`p\` values.
3. Verify total path probability equals one, sign symmetry and
   matching one-time noise moments. Test that the two values really
   differ, despite identical scalar proxies.
4. Check the elementary white-noise stationary limit against
   \`s2/(1-a^2)\`; check both unique fixed points by substituting
   them directly into the two coupled moment equations.
5. Add adversarial tests: reject probability outside [0,1], wrong
   noise distribution, erroneous 12-step count, altered transition
   weights, and an incorrect candidate fixed point. Use exact
   fractions for all scientific decisions; floats only for labeled
   human-readable approximations.
6. Record full fractions, recurrence trace at 12 and 40 steps,
   exact 12-step oracle values, assumption boundaries,
   source SHA and CI links. Do not claim observed stochastic
   measurements, independent field replication, or data-driven fit.
