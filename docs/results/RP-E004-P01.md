**Status: INSTRUMENTATION FEASIBILITY PILOT — STRUCTURAL PASS,
WITH AUDITABILITY LIMITATIONS.**
This result is about a tiny controlled HTTP work-queue service,
**not** observed natural-system physics or a validation of
`C = B/(I*tau)`.

## Original measured values

All measurements are of the **same local software queue and runner**.
Work units are accepted HTTP tasks; calibration is the *completed*
tasks per second in a separate saturated pretrial interval.
Intensity is delivered work rate during the timed intervention.
Recovery is the first *sampled* zero-backlog/idle status after
the injection ended, followed by the programmed 500-ms dwell check.

| sham-01 | — | 0 | No action | No positive-dose endpoint | Undefined | Sham as specified |
| positive-02 | 81.985985 | 270.791931 | 0.072620598 | 0.341000489 | 4.169117 | Admissible |

The observed trial order was `positive-01`, `sham-01`,
`positive-02`. Each positive injector reported **120 successful
HTTP task deliveries**. The two controllers separately recorded
actions; the sham controller recorded **no action**. Both positive
monitors reported recovery before their eight-second deadline.
The two positive injections used the **same nominal treatment**; the
differences above reflect operational timing variation, not a
preregistered dose-response experiment.

The exact two measured candidate-index values are shown only to
demonstrate successful units/field handling. **Neither is tested as a
predictor of Y**, and their small variation cannot demonstrate any
association or mechanism.

positive-01 STRUCTURALLY_ADMISSIBLE

The run passed the **programmed structural gate**. This is sufficient
to justify designing a more thoroughly audited instrumentation study.
It does **not** certify actual calibration saturation beyond the
two recorded queue snapshots, accuracy of the OS clock, independent
hardware sensors, or external physical generality.

## Observed audit gaps — do not conceal

1. **Monitor trace not retained in full:** the original monitor wrote
   number of samples, first recovery timestamp and observation-end
   timestamp, **not every sampled backlog/idle value or sampling
   interval**. The program checked its internal sample sequence, but
   an independent reviewer cannot reconstruct the 500-ms sustained
   idle series from the retained first-run logs alone. Consequently,
   the observed recovery result is **not independently trace-replayable**.
   This is an instrumentation audit limitation, not a negative test of C.
2. **One host, not independent instruments:** the four role loggers
   were separate *processes*, but shared one kernel's clock, CPU and
   localhost service. They can share scheduling interference and
   error sources. Distinct logger IDs are not proof of causal
   independence.
3. **Capacity at one operating regime:** calibration captured
   41 completed tasks during approximately 0.5 seconds with a
   nonempty queue at both sampled boundaries. It is a measured
   local work rate, not a device-wide maximum independently certified
   against alternative load generators.
4. **Control mechanism by design:** the controller sets a worker
   service interval from 12 ms to 6 ms, so backlog reduction is
   explicable through classical queue mechanics and the intervention.
   There is no novel explanatory success hidden in this outcome.
5. **Tiny sample and censoring power:** two positive trials and
   a sham are inadequate for a statistical relation between C
   and persistence. Failed-to-recover events and inter-host noise
   remain untested in this first pilot.
6. **Units:** because B and I use the same work-unit/s convention
   while tau uses seconds, candidate C is in 1/s. This differs
   from RP-001's noise-variance convention and is not a
   cross-domain scalar unless another mapping is justified.

**Independent scientific conclusion:** this run establishes that the
proposed software instrumentation can collect structurally admissible
timing/queue records in a simple controlled environment. It provides
**zero evidence for or against a general persistence law**.
The next preregistration must preserve per-probe timestamps/backlog
values, verify actual sustained recovery by independent log replay,
probe clock jitter and treatment nonresponse, and compare against
queue-conservation baselines before making a predictive claim.

## Source and contribution

The FIFO queue, work conservation, response delays and time-to-drain
behavior follow ordinary software systems and queueing principles.
The contribution of RP-E004-P01 is the **predeclared identification,
control, instrumentation and auditable STOP/PROCEED structure**.
No priority or physical novelty claim is made.
