# Twenty-two small, falsifiable questions about cellular, instrumental and neural persistence

**Literature reconnaissance:** 9 November 2026  
**Purpose:** generate precise **research candidates**, not claim new laws, previously unseen work or clinical translation.

**Novelty status convention.** **GAP** means a published review or dataset description explicitly identifies the broader problem as unresolved. **CANDIDATE** means the narrower experiment is potentially distinctive but an equivalent earlier paper has **not** been ruled out by a systematic bibliographic search. Neither label means *nobody else did this*. Each item must first pass a focused authors/DOI and methods prior-art search and source eligibility check, then a registered test with negative and competing baselines.

The user's correction of the scientific goal is preserved in [the research charter](CHARTER-REALITY-NOT-UNIVERSAL-LAW-2026-11-09.md): **truthful domain-specific answers with value for humanity, not a universal law**.

## A. Molecular oncology — preserve healthy tissue rather than simply maximize tumor-cell survival

**ONC-01 — Does a residual cell remember drug exposure, or does its niche remember?**  
**Small question:** In one published longitudinal lineage experiment, do pre-existing clonal identity and an explicitly measured tumour-cell state before a later event predict reactivation better than contemporaneous neighbour/microenvironment features, with test lineages held out? **Pilot:** audit [GSE234174](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE234174) for actual matched lineage, state and niche fields before claiming this is possible; if no linked niche/lineage temporal data, return **NOT IDENTIFIABLE**. **Controls:** lineage-only, niche-only, time-only, batch and pre-existing clone. **Status: GAP in joint longitudinal state/niche evidence; precise test CANDIDATE.** Already studied broadly: [review of post-therapy cellular memory (2026)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13678069/).

**ONC-02 — Does inflammatory *temporal pattern* add predictive information beyond dose/exposure?**  
**Small question:** When cumulative exposure to a specified inflammation-linked signal is controlled, does its temporal autocorrelation or number of pulses predict awakening of dormant disseminated cells better than exposure total? **Pilot:** first determine whether experimental temporal stimulation and later awakening outcomes are jointly available, then fit a pulse-memory and total-exposure baseline on independent replicate systems; **do not invent treatment trials from one static RNA snapshot**. **Status: CANDIDATE; related inflammatory triggering is already established** by Pereira et al., “Inflammatory cytokines mediate the induction of and awakening from metastatic dormancy” (2025), DOI [10.1016/j.celrep.2025.115388](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2025.115388). The question is the incremental *time-structure* contrast, not the claim inflammation matters.

**ONC-03 — When does therapy-induced senescence cease to protect and begin to support recurrence?**  
**Small question:** Within one genotype and a single defined therapy, is change in **duration** of a senescence-associated secretory programme more predictive of later cell re-entry/immune clearance than initial programme magnitude, and does any threshold reproduce across models? **Pilot:** test availability of repeated functional assays, rather than using a single p21/p16-positive specimen as proof of reversible senescence. **Controls:** cell death, senescence-induced initial arrest, immune clearance, proliferation and clone selection. **Status: GAP explicitly acknowledged** in the 2026 review [Therapy-Induced Senescence in Cancer Treatment Resistance](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13664522/). No existing universal switch time is assumed.

**ONC-04 — Are differences between organ niches stronger than a shared dormancy expression score?**  
**Small question:** Does a candidate cell-state score trained for dormant lung disseminated cells systematically fail on otherwise comparable bone-derived dormant cells **after** matching cell lineage and sampling phase? **Pilot:** dataset/metadata audit for matched tissue, lineage, time and state; if absent, stop instead of pretending to validate a cross-organ model. **Controls:** tissue identity alone and held-out donor/source. **Status: GAP in organ-specific mechanisms; exact cross-niche comparison CANDIDATE.** Context: [Cell biology of cancer dormancy (2026)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42713715/).

**ONC-05 — Do early signs of normal-tissue immune surveillance outperform tumour-cell-only warning markers?**  
**Small question:** On independently held-out longitudinal studies, does an *antecedent* host-cell inflammatory or stromal response improve an awakening prediction beyond the most recent tumour-cell measurement? **Pilot:** precheck feature release times and use patient/clone- or animal-group splits. **Controls:** host-only, tumour-only, sampling interval, batch, lesion site, and leakage from post-awakening biopsies. **Status: CANDIDATE**; [intrinsic/extrinsic dormancy review (2026)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13250524/) already identifies several microenvironmental mechanisms.

**ONC-06 — Can a non-ionizing experimental strategy delay progression without increasing a persistent malignant reservoir?**  
**Small question:** In a *specific preclinical context*, compare a reported non-ionizing tumour-control intervention with its validated control on **two endpoints simultaneously**: tumour burden and residual reactivation-capable clone fraction. **Pilot:** a retrospective evidence matrix of experiments for which both endpoints and follow-up are measured, not invention of treatments or dosing protocols. **Failure:** reduced short-term burden with increased viable dormant reservoir. **Status: CANDIDATE; mechanism- and cancer-specific.** [National Cancer Institute treatment overview](https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/treatment/types) confirms multiple established therapy types; this question **never** licenses avoiding clinically indicated radiation treatment or stopping a patient's therapy.

## B. Cytology and epithelial defence — which functions must survive?

**CYT-01 — Can geometry distinguish reversible quiescence from long-term senescence, beyond single-cell gene expression?**  
**Small question:** At a fixed sampling time and drug exposure, do lineage/cell-cycle dynamics improve classification of future cell-cycle re-entry over transcriptomic measurements alone? **Pilot:** inspect which longitudinal time-lapse labels and molecular specimens in the original [GSE297393](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE297393) series are truly alignable. The source describes **MCF10A human epithelial cells** after etoposide, not a validated clinical cancer cohort. **Status: CANDIDATE**, adjacent to the original study already distinguishing heterogeneous arrest states. No “new quiescence continuum” claim.

**CYT-02 — Is epithelial elimination determined only by the fraction of altered neighbours or also by *their spatial arrangement*?**  
**Small question:** Under equal cell-type proportions and mechanical conditions, do arrangements with one contiguous clone versus several separated clones yield different extrusion/competitive outcomes? **Pilot:** first test this identifiability in an explicit **synthetic cell-contact graph**; later require live-imaging data. **Controls:** contact number, shape, clone area, cell division and single-cell intrinsic fitness. **Status: CANDIDATE**, not a discovery of cell competition. Prior art: [Epithelial guardians against cancer (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12672-025-03519-7), [Biophysical principles of cell competition (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tcb.2026.04.009).

**CYT-03 — Could healthy tissue maintain its function by deliberately losing individual cells?**  
**Small question:** In a defined epithelial model, is functional barrier integrity better preserved by *timely* elimination of damaged cells than by maximizing total living-cell count? **Pilot:** define independent barrier and extrusion measurements, then compare trajectories over a fixed interval, adjusting for cell-density recovery. **Negative control:** high viable-cell number without preserved barrier integrity. **Status: CANDIDATE**. The broader function of extrusion and competition is established prior art: [2025 epithelial defence review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12511482/).

**CYT-04 — What counts as ‘recovery’ if the same surviving cells change their identities?**  
**Small question:** After a standard cell stress, do neighbourhood relationships and functional marker restoration predict subsequent barrier recovery beyond the fraction of cells that simply survive? **Pilot:** require longitudinal single-cell or imaging plus independent barrier function, otherwise stop at a descriptive model. **Controls:** survival-only, proliferation, and cell-population replacement. **Status: CANDIDATE**; distinguish *functional persistence* from *component persistence* without universalizing the distinction.

## C. ARARA — separate a real sky change from an internal instrument fault

**ARA-01 — Can image and health telemetry contradict each other in a way that helps classify the cause?**  
**Small question:** In a preregistered set of *simulated paired* astronomical frames and detector-health signals, does combining the channels reduce **false attribution** of sensor faults as sky events compared with strong image-only real/bogus and telemetry-only fault-detection baselines? **Pilot:** evaluate channel removal and simultaneous independent sky+instrument anomalies. **Crucial feasibility caveat:** [OPSSAT-AD](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-025-05035-3) provides actual satellite telemetry anomalies while [astronomical artefact datasets](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1384107625001162) contain image-based examples. They are **not a matched joint observation dataset**; do not concatenate records as if they belonged to one instrument. **Status: CANDIDATE**, joint domain dataset not established.

**ARA-02 — Can a misclassified source create a *self-amplifying instrumental failure*?**  
**Small question:** In a transparent simulated pointing/confirmation loop, how many false source confirmations are needed before the controller starts increasing its own pointing error? **Pilot:** compare an unguarded controller with one requiring an independent confirmation channel and measure mispointing, alert loss and false correction. **Status: CANDIDATE**, but **the general phenomenon is not new**: the [Gaia First Look instrument-health paper (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202659204) documents false detections entering pointing control after a CCD unit problem.

**ARA-03 — When are internal and external causes *provably indistinguishable* from available measurements?**  
**Small question:** Construct two finite causal measurement models—one external transient, one detector perturbation—with **identical optical measurements**. Determine the *smallest additional independent signal* that makes the causes distinguishable, or report no identifiability under the sensor budget. **Pilot:** exhaustive finite observation/latent-state enumeration with explicit noise assumptions, not a trained classifier. **Status: CANDIDATE**; the mathematical framework of state estimation and fault isolation already exists, so novelty would depend on a previously uncovered telescope-specific observation limit.

**ARA-04 — Can the instrument recognise when its confidence is no longer trustworthy?**  
**Small question:** Under a frozen detector change (new point-spread function, dead-pixel pattern or elevated background), does a predeclared **abstention** rule reduce false astronomical alerts *without losing too many real injections* compared with calibrated image-only and conformal/OOD baselines? **Pilot:** inject events into held-out detector conditions and report calibration, false discovery, miss rate and abstention burden, not accuracy alone. **Status: CANDIDATE**. The [Fink/Rubin alert-quality documentation](https://doc.lsst.fink-broker.org/data/quality/) warns against overtrusting early real/bogus reliability scores.

**ARA-05 — Which observations remain stable in sky coordinates but not on a detector?**  
**Small question:** Under controlled dithering/pointing changes, does a candidate follow sky coordinates across independent exposures rather than remaining in one detector pixel/column? **Pilot:** compare basic geometric consistency, difference-image real/bogus and telemetry-fusion methods on injections that include coincident true transients and detector defects. **Status: CANDIDATE; the geometric consistency principle and real/bogus classification are known**, so novelty must be in demonstrated incremental reliability and calibrated safety under joint anomalies. See [Nature Astronomy transient classification (2025)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41550-025-02670-z).

**ARARA scope note:** The [ARARA archive](https://github.com/DevHabito/arara-space-telescope) preserves an experimental computational programme. Its README explicitly states it is **not** a validated operational telescope or real transient detection pipeline; no result above should pretend otherwise.

## D. Brain, Self and psychopathology — continuity, adaptability and body–world distinction

**NEU-01 — Is Lempel–Ziv complexity really tracking flexible neural activity, or measurement/preprocessing choices?**  
**Small question:** After fixing recording length and behavioural condition, does an apparent individual LZc difference survive equalized band-limited power, aperiodic spectral content, artifact rejection, binarization thresholds and surrogate time-series controls? **Pilot:** preregister one EEG processing pipeline on [OpenNeuro ds004148](https://nemar.org/dataset/on004148) or a more suitable original dataset. **Comparator:** spectral power, 1/f background, basic entropy, preprocessing-only artifact baselines. **Status: GAP in clinical interpretability of dynamical markers; exact pipeline ablation CANDIDATE.** Prior art: [A dynamical systems framework for precision psychiatry (2025)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-025-01984-6).

**NEU-02 — Can two people with similar LZc have different capacities to *switch states and recover*?**  
**Small question:** In a genuine within-person cognitive-task dataset, after controlling mean LZc, does a measured state transition/recovery parameter independently predict later task performance or stability? **Pilot:** [OpenNeuro/NEMAR ds004148](https://nemar.org/dataset/on004148) describes 60 people and repeated rest/task states; verify actual chronology and trial-level functional outcomes before analysis. **Comparator:** LZc alone, frequency spectrum, baseline performance, random baseline; hold out subjects. **Status: CANDIDATE**, closely related to existing metastability research; [2025 systematic scoping review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40090532/) highlights weak task applicability and inconsistent definitions.

**NEU-03 — Can the brain distinguish a changed body from changed outside stimuli without losing self-relevant continuity?**  
**Small question:** Do pre-stimulus physiological features (ECG, respiration) improve discrimination between **interoceptive heartbeat attention** and a matched external auditory control beyond EEG alone, and is the effect stable across sessions? **Pilot:** inspect original paired conditions and labels in [EEG/ECG interoception–exteroception dataset (Zenodo 2025)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17953246). **No claim:** attention classification is not an objective measure of consciousness or self-identity. **Status: CANDIDATE**; [embodied DMN interpretation (2025)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2352154625001263) already makes internal-model and interoceptive links.

**NEU-04 — Are network dynamics a stable *personal characteristic* and a changing *state* at the same time?**  
**Small question:** For a fixed EEG measure, independently estimate within-person retest reliability **and** task-related responsiveness, then compare those two properties; does a combined feature perform better on future tasks than either alone? **Pilot:** [OpenNeuro ds004148](https://nemar.org/dataset/on004148), with explicit recorded state sequence and test–retest sessions. **Status: GAP in biomarkers combining stability with useful sensitivity, narrow design CANDIDATE.** Existing relevant results: [2025 EEG microstate sequence retest study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41383564/) and [2026 brain metastability reliability study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13108507/). Do not call test–retest itself a new idea.

**NEU-05 — Can neural information complexity be related to an *actual measured energy cost*?**  
**Small question:** When oxygen/glucose use or a validated metabolic measurement is present, does LZc add prediction of task performance after controlling that measured cost and physiological confounders? **Pilot:** first audit whether one dataset truly has same-person neural signals, an explicit metabolic measurement and outcome. If not, return **NO DIRECT PHYSICAL ENERGY IDENTIFICATION**; do not equate information-theoretic LZc, variational free energy and measured thermodynamic energy. **Status: GAP at conceptual-to-physical measurement bridge; exact empirical target CANDIDATE.** Related [7T allostatic–interoceptive network mapping (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-025-02087-x) establishes network relevance but not metabolic equivalence.

**NEU-06 — Do the reported ‘rigidity/disintegration’ measurements replicate beyond a tiny pilot?**  
**Small question:** Apply the original LZc+phase-synchrony calculation, with its parameters frozen, to larger independent individual-level data, reporting whether the claimed diagnostic separation remains after controls for source preprocessing, age, medicine, arousal and site. **Pilot:** first audit access and labels; report missing clinical labels honestly, not infer diagnosis from EEG. **Status: direct necessary replication, not a new biomarker claim.** The investigator's [2025 theoretical preprint](https://philarchive.org/rec/ROMTTO-5) and [2026 MEG proof of concept](https://philarchive.org/rec/ROMTTO-6) exist; the latter explicitly reports **N=1 per group**, far too small for establishing group-level clinical accuracy or a validated biomarker.

**NEU-07 — Does measured flexibility predict the *subjective continuity of self* better than either complexity or synchrony alone?**  
**Small question:** Where ethically collected first-person continuity/agency ratings **already coexist** with interoception/exteroception conditions, test whether transition metrics predict **future within-person** ratings after measured arousal, performance and recording quality are controlled. **Pilot:** evidence gate first; never invent self-report from a meditative-depth label. [OpenNeuro ds008836](https://openneuro.org/datasets/ds008836/versions/1.0.2/metadata) has EEG and repeated meditation depth ratings but *depth is not the same outcome as self-continuity*. **Status: CANDIDATE requiring specific behavioural measurement and potentially independent ethical approval.**

## E. Two interdisciplinary comparisons that do not assume a common law

**BRIDGE-01 — What is the minimal evidence needed to separate ‘inside’ from ‘outside’ causal change in each field?**  
First build **three different** generative examples—instrument fault versus sky transient, cell-intrinsic memory versus niche-induced dormancy, and body-state versus external-stimulus inference. For each, construct one pair of observationally indistinguishable scenarios; then ask which *domain-specific* additional measurement breaks that ambiguity. **A negative finding (non-identifiability with available sensors) is a useful result.** No equation is presumed to govern all domains.

**BRIDGE-02 — How should a system decide when *not* to act under uncertainty?**  
For ARARA, abstaining can preserve a real discovery while avoiding a false alert. For oncology, deferring an experimental research intervention may avoid selecting resistant clones—but **this is not treatment guidance**. For brain biomarkers, abstaining from a diagnostic classification can prevent overclaiming. Small first test: explicit domain-specific cost matrix and calibrated confidence thresholds versus no-abstention; **never** compare absolute scores across fields without matching outcome units or consequences.

## What the sources already resolved, and what remains open

**Established prior art:** inflammatory modulation of dormancy, cell competition/extrusion, Lempel–Ziv and metastability analysis, astronomical real/bogus and spacecraft anomaly diagnostics. Identical named approaches are **not** new.

**Open or disputed in checked reviews:** dynamic and clinically meaningful dormancy state/rewakening definitions, the time at which senescence becomes detrimental, joint lineage–niche–rechallenge evidence, metastability construct/retest/task validity, biophysical interpretation of brain dynamic biomarkers, and low-false-alarm classification under instrument drift.

**Unknown under this reconnaissance:** whether any *exact* ONC/CYT/ARA/NEU question was already addressed in a source not found in this selective search. Do **not** claim scientific priority without dedicated systematic searches per item, including methods, supplemental material, data and preprints. The scope is one source-guided **question portfolio**, not a completed systematic review.

## Publication gate for each question

For every chosen entry, register: exact literature search/date/query and DOI list; verified original data access and license; source-specific observations and predecision information; baseline methods from *the actual nearest papers*; one primary falsifier and expected sign; held-out split by person/lineage/instrument/detector where relevant; nuisance controls; negative control; missingness criterion; calibrated error and uncertainty; and immutable first-run output, including failures. Only after these conditions are met may we test the proposed additional signal. A stable negative answer is scientifically valuable.

**No universal law is a deliverable or success criterion.** The central goal is a correct, helpful, narrowly bounded answer about reality.
