# Research scope and nonclaims

## Objective

Investigate which relational structures and measurable mechanisms permit functional coordination to survive or recover after perturbation. Begin with models small enough to prove, disprove, or enumerate exactly. Advance to independent observations only when the measurement contract is credible.

## Levels of evidence

1. **Definition:** a declared map from inputs to a number; true by construction but not predictive.
2. **Deductive result:** a statement proved under explicit assumptions; independent of empirical truth.
3. **Finite numerical result:** tested for named parameters and tolerances; cannot certify generality.
4. **Observed-structure simulation:** a real network with hypothetical dynamics; not measured behavior.
5. **Empirical evaluation:** measured inputs and independent outcomes, with predeclared tests and baseline comparisons.
6. **Physical hypothesis:** a possible interpretation subject to further measurement, invariance, and discriminating predictions.

Never promote an item between levels merely because the tests passed.

## Two separate research questions

**Q1: Scalar sufficiency.** Do capacity, noise and delay suffice to predict operational persistence within a specified class? RP-001 probes one precise claim about Q1.

**Q2: Derivation.** Can a selected persistence observable be derived from independently motivated relational dynamics without tuning assumptions to desired outcomes? Q2 is presently open.

Even if Q1 fails, Q2 may yield restricted-domain theorems. A different model may require topology, spectra, interaction signs, memory, adversarial noise, or correlations. Their omission must be justified, never hidden in a free scale factor.

## Unknowns

- No unique, empirically calibrated cross-domain persistence observable has been selected.
- No universal reduction from the PMFF primitives `(R, q, mu, K, pi)` to `(B, I, tau)` exists in the current record.
- No physical emergence of spacetime, gravity or matter is established by these experiments.
- No new fundamental law or priority claim is made.
- Independence of noise, stationarity and linear evolution are temporary model assumptions and must be stress-tested.

## Failure taxonomy

`FAIL-COMPUTATION`: code, data, or invariants disagree; fix before interpreting.  
`FAIL-MODEL`: prediction disagrees with a well-defined observable for a declared domain.  
`UNDERIDENTIFIED`: alternative parameterizations share observations.  
`INCONCLUSIVE`: evidence is too weak or measurement is ambiguous.  
`SUPPORTED-WITHIN-SCOPE`: an independent test passes without extending the claim.

Never call model failure a computational bug unless a demonstrated software defect exists.
