# Research charter — answers to reality, not a universal law

**9 November 2026** · Authorial research direction articulated by the investigator.

## Purpose

We are **not** searching for or presupposing a universal law of nature. We are searching for **truthful, rigorously testable, potentially useful answers to concrete questions about reality**, even when those answers turn out to be local, conditional, negative, unoriginal, or counter to our expectations. The intended human benefit matters, but it must never override the evidence.

This corrects the overly broad "hidden universal law" framing sometimes attached to the relational-persistence investigation. Do not represent the investigator as committed to finding a law that explains everything, or treat failure to find such a law as failure of the research.

The shared curiosity is how a particular organized system distinguishes changes caused by its **own sensing, internal state or response** from changes in its surroundings, and how its relevant **function** survives, fails, adapts or recovers. This may yield **different** answers in each field.

## Independent research programmes

- **Oncology and molecular biology:** preservation of *normal tissue/organism function* is not the same as preservation of cancer-cell survival. Investigate dormancy, microenvironmental regulation, clonal resistance, immune surveillance and prevention or control with evidence-based non-ionizing therapeutic strategies as possible topics. Clinical treatment decisions remain outside this computational research.
- **Cell biology and cytology:** understand when healthy epithelia eliminate altered neighbours and which cell–cell, mechanical and metabolic interactions establish tissue function. Normal tissue fitness is the relevant endpoint, not survival of every cell.
- **ARARA Space Telescope:** distinguish genuinely external astronomical phenomena from instrument/detector/processing failures, including uncertain or causally ambiguous cases. Current ARARA code is a historical computational research archive; it is **not** an operating observatory or physically validated astronomical detector.
- **Neuroscience and the Self:** study stability, adaptability, self-relevant/interoceptive processing, network dynamics and psychophysiological measurements. The investigator's *The Thermodynamics of Self* is a theoretical preprint, and a subsequent pilot reports N=1 per diagnostic group; they do **not** establish a validated individual clinical biomarker or identify statistical Lempel–Ziv complexity with thermodynamic entropy.

No shared metric is mandatory. Cross-domain comparison is permitted **only after** the domain-specific observables, baseline models and failure conditions are independently established.

## Non-negotiable epistemic rules

1. **Existing work is prior art.** Dormancy, cell competition, state transitions, dynamic fault detection, real/bogus astronomical classification, brain metastability and EEG Lempel–Ziv complexity all have substantial prior literatures. New *word combinations* are not novelty.
2. **Unanswered does not mean unstudied.** Distinguish an unresolved issue explicitly acknowledged in a credible review from a narrow candidate question for which an exact prior study has **not yet been excluded**. The latter requires systematic literature checking before any priority statement.
3. **Measurement is not mechanism.** A cell-expression snapshot is not lineage history; an optical artefact classifier is not a measured onboard fault trace; LZ complexity is not calorimetry; inferred functional connectivity is not an experimentally identified neural circuit; event-time labels are not necessarily predecision information.
4. **No clinical inference without clinical evidence.** Tumour dormancy can precede relapse. Alternatives to ionizing radiation exist but no research hypothesis licenses withholding prescribed radiotherapy, cessation of cancer treatment or an individual risk claim. No new human/animal experiment without independent ethical approval.
5. **Define whose function we protect.** Tumour persistence and tissue health may conflict. Adaptive brain state transitions are not equivalent to low disorder scores. Rejecting an astronomical alert may avert false discovery or cause loss of a real phenomenon.
6. **Preserve null and negative answers.** Register hypothesis, fixed source version, denominator, proxy and comparison baseline before looking at primary outcomes; publish failure, missing data, contradictory evidence, and inability to establish identifiability.
7. **Causality needs identification.** A correlation between signals does not prove internal versus external origin. Observed state differences do not establish response to a counterfactual intervention; a simulation does not establish field efficacy.
8. **Protect scope.** Do not assert that all 22 candidate questions in the accompanying portfolio have never been addressed in literature. They are candidate **research niches** with explicit neighbouring prior art, not certified exclusive scientific priority.

## Scientific aim

Produce discrete, useful, reproducible answers that are accurate within their documented boundaries. Examples of meaningful contributions include showing a claimed biomarker fails when preprocessing is controlled; identifying which instrument telemetry channels are actually necessary to reject a false sky event; isolating a source of tumour-dormancy state persistence in a well-defined model; or discovering that an apparently plausible inference is **not identifiable** from the available data.

The research advances by learning **what reality permits us to say**, not by insisting that nature must obey a preferred grand theory.
