Both strict comma candidates raised an error, precisely matching the earlier instrumental abort. Those first-header results were known before D06 was designed.
- The **D06 protocol** was committed *after* D04/D05, but *before D06 read a new original CSV record*, freezing the only permitted parser change: independently handle delimiter candidates so a bad **alternative** cannot veto a valid **selected** separator. It also froze the original D04 **whole compressed file digests** as required bytes for replay. The rest of the parsing, budget, CRC/EOF and field requirements remained restrictive.

This is not a blind discovery or physical replication. It is a transparent second source-quality test using **identical pinned source objects** with an independently diagnosed software defect corrected.

## Strong restrictions on what these data prove

This first complete source-format scan does **not** establish:

- distinct emergency entity counts, duplicate rates, original `CodOcorrencia` to `CodOcorrenciaEmergencial` join cardinality, or number of unmatched interruptions;
- independent validation of reported timestamps, event types, consumer counts, or legitimate **decision-time** availability of a feature;
- reconstruction of the live energized distribution graph or repairs from annual BDGD feeder/substation identifiers;
- assignment of repair strategies, eligible alternative actions, counterfactual outcomes or observed decision CPU/crew costs;
- the physical applicability of the synthetic RP-U004 two-vertex-disjoint **F2** criterion to a typically radial distribution network;
- a causal advantage of two-step lookahead, a cross-domain law, a new mathematical theorem or the scalar `C=B/(I*tau)`.

A correct dataset decoder is not proof that any model describes its physical system. The genuine achievement here is a reproducible, instrument-corrected structural inventory of two third-party operational files, including their negative/missing-data facts.
