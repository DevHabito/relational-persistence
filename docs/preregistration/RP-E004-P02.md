# RP-E004-P02 — frozen auditability and action-clock instrumentation pilot

**Preregistration status: FROZEN BEFORE ANY P02 MEASUREMENT.** This is a
new feasibility experiment, not a revision of P01, an observational
external-system replication, or a test of the physical hypothesis
`C = B/(I*tau)`. The sole primary decision is whether the new instruments
produce **replayable, temporally coherent** records under four fixed conditions.

## Prior result and precise deficiency

RP-E004-P01 passed its *programmed* structural gate on a small single-host
HTTP queue, but its health monitor saved a recovery timestamp and number of
samples **without retaining every underlying health sample**. Its controller
logged a successful client-side action acknowledgement rather than an
independent service-side application timestamp. Neither deficiency may
be repaired retrospectively. See the frozen P01 protocol and its report.

## Fixed service and intervention

- Run on a single hosted Ubuntu GitHub Actions runner, using localhost
  traffic only. A distinct service process owns one FIFO queue capped at
  512 tasks, with one worker sleeping initially about **12 ms/task**.
  The controller threshold is observed queued depth **> 5**, sampled
  every **25 ms**; a successful control request asks the service to use
  **6 ms/task** for subsequently started work.
- One independent process per calibration, injector, controller, health
  probe. They are distinct Linux processes but **not independent hardware**
  and share a Linux `time.monotonic_ns` clock domain.
- Four fresh-service trials, shuffled deterministically with Python
  random seed **20261024**: **2 positive enabled-controller**,
  **1 positive controller-disabled**, **1 sham**.
- Each positive injector attempts **120** local HTTP tasks, spacing
  requests by **3 ms**; the actually *accepted* count and observed
  first-onset/last-finish times are logged. Sham injects exactly
  **0** tasks over nominal **360 ms**. A rejected HTTP task, failed
  process, or timeout is reported; nothing is silently imputed.
- The disabled-controller positive trial delivers the **same positive
  workload** without any worker-interval change. Controller action time
  and candidate index are explicitly **undefined**; its observed
  recovery may be seen or **right-censored** and is never discarded.
  This is an instrumentation negative control, **not** a comparator
  for testing C.
- Recovery is **first observed** queued depth = 0 and active workers
  = 0 *after the logged injection end*, followed by at least
  **500 ms** of sampled idle observations with **no observed nonidle
  sample**. The monitor follows up to **8 s** after injection end.
  No claim is made about unobserved instants between samples.
- Each monitor must preserve every GET `/status` reply as append-only
  JSONL including probe timestamp, service-side snapshot timestamp,
  queued, active and completed. Intended sample cadence **25 ms**;
  **any** adjacent sample gap exceeding **150 ms** after injection end
  and before monitoring finishes is a stop, not silently interpolated.
- To avoid circular recovery measurement, the monitor derives the
  endpoint only from observed health samples and the injector's
  independent end marker, **not B, I, tau, or control parameters**.

## Capacity calibration and clock checks

- Each fresh service first performs a sparse control workload of
  **12 work units at nominal 40 ms spacing** and separately logs
  its actual completion behavior. This is *not* a capacity estimate.
- An independent pretrial calibrator then enqueues **180 tasks**,
  records queue/active/completed snapshots at nominal
  **0, 0.25, 0.50, 0.75 s**, and reports **three** saturated
  throughput windows. Every window requires positive queue at both
  endpoints and positive completed-work delta.
- Define `B` for this pilot as summed completed work across the
  three windows divided by their total observed elapsed seconds;
  report each window and variation separately. If the
  largest-window rate differs by more than **25% of the mean**
  from another window, stop rather than retune.
- Calibration and sparse probe must drain completely before treatment.
  Report observed queue snapshots: a busy queue supports *local
  saturation*, **not** globally maximal capacity in all regimes.
- The controller must timestamp request **send** and response
  **receive**, and the service must timestamp the actual worker-speed
  setting change *under its own lock*. For enabled interventions,
  require `send_ns <= service_apply_ns <= acknowledge_ns`, and
  `acknowledge_ns - send_ns <= 150 ms`; violation is a STOP.
- The measured `tau` is `service_apply_ns - injector_onset_ns`,
  not configured worker sleep, polling cadence, client ack, or Y.
  Report ack latency separately. No relation of tau to a natural
  physical response mechanism is claimed.

## Frozen data dictionary

`B`: local completed HTTP work units/s during three pretrial
saturated windows. `I`: successfully accepted positive local HTTP
work units divided by actual injector duration, units/s.
`tau`: service-side action timestamp minus externally logged injector
onset timestamp, seconds. `Y`: first *sampled* sustained recovery
time minus injector end timestamp, seconds, or right-censored at 8 s.
The candidate quotient `B/(I*tau)` has units **1/s** under
this pilot's work-rate convention; it is **undefined** for
sham and controller-disabled trials. Do not compare it with P01's
values as if both measurements used interchangeable protocols.

## Frozen feasibility decision

**PASS_AUDITABLE_INSTRUMENTATION** if and only if:

1. All four fresh-server trials complete and retain distinct
   calibrator/injector/controller/health-process IDs and event logs.
2. Both positive enabled-controller trials pass the existing
   RP-E003 `validate_evidence` gate, plus the three-window
   saturation and internal-service-action ordering audits, and
   produce replayably witnessed 500-ms idle recovery within 8 s.
3. Positive disabled-controller trial delivers at least one task,
   logs *no* controller action, leaves C undefined, and reports an
   independently replayable recovered-or-right-censored outcome.
4. Sham delivers zero tasks and logs no controller action; C undefined.
5. All four monitor JSONL streams can be replayed **independently**
   from raw timestamps, queue/active values and the injector end event.
   The replay must produce the same censoring/first recovery time
   as the monitor. No after-end poll gap may exceed **150 ms**.
6. All 3 calibration windows are auditable and pass the registered
   relative variation tolerance (25%); all raw logs have reproducible
   SHA-256 digests.
7. No crashed process, missing role, missing event, timestamp inversion,
   invalid clock domain, queue overflow, or failed local HTTP request.

If **any** condition fails, decision =
`STOP_INSTRUMENTATION_UNRELIABLE`. A failed trial is retained in the
summary with its raw log artifacts and cannot be replaced or cherry-picked
to obtain a pass. No statistical or hypothesis-test result about
C vs Y is permitted in either outcome.

## Reproducibility and limits

- Standard-library Python 3.12, bounded localhost queue and no
  external telemetry or data imports beyond package installation.
  Hosted workflow total runtime cap **10 minutes**.
- Publish protocol hash, git SHA, role timestamps, full sampled trace,
  per-window calibration, event SHA-256, Python/kernel versions,
  mono-clock resolution and eventual decision. Retain failed logs.
- Adversarial tests must reject tampered, missing, out-of-order,
  slow-gapped and contradictory monitor samples, controller ack-before-
  action, missing service action, and invalid calibration windows.
- Successful replay means **auditable sampled evidence**, not a
  continuous real-time proof of idle between probes, independent
  physical instruments, causal identification, or a new law of
  persistence. Any subsequent predictive study needs a **new**
  preregistration with classical queueing baseline, proper sample
  size and independent systems.

**Prior literature:** FIFO queues, workload intensity, monotonic
clocks and controller delay belong to standard systems engineering.
The project contribution is the protocol-specific, falsifiable
measurement-quality gate. This document asserts no novelty in
queueing theory or hardware measurement.
