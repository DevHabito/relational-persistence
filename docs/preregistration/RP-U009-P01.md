# RP-U009-P01 — Registered real compressor pressure around documented maintenance events

**17 November 2026: source-free preregistration.** No RP-U009 original MetroPT-3 CSV values, complete records, pressure analyses, maintenance-window counts or outcome statistics have been read at this freeze. The UCI dataset metadata and its publicly printed maintenance/failure table **have** been read and are explicitly disclosed. This is a bounded first physical-asset data feasibility/pressure audit, not a causal maintenance study.

## Motivation and previous negative evidence

RP-U007 found **no registered increment** from a live software queue measurement over a substantially informed baseline. RP-U008 found four previously published local network outages in an externally gathered dataset: two had HTTP recovery one sampling row after gateway reachability. That n=4, single-collector result is **not known physical repair** and cannot imply a relational law. We now require a physical device with both recorded operating sensors and **separate reported maintenance events**. No prior software toy is counted as a physical replication.

The candidate is *MetroPT-3*, real rolling-stock air-production compressor telemetry, originally distributed by Davari et al. and documented by Veloso et al. in *Scientific Data* (2022), DOI **10.1038/s41597-022-01877-3**. The official UCI release is **dataset 791**, DOI **10.24432/C5VW3R**, [archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/791/metropt+3+dataset](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/791/metropt%2B3%2B), licensed CC BY 4.0. The official URL selected for the first original source is exactly:

`https://archive.ics.uci.edu/static/public/791/metropt%2B3%2Bdataset.zip`

The UCI description identifies **1,516,948** samples and a bundled CSV named `MetroPT3(AirCompressor).csv` (approximately 208.2 MB uncompressed), with `timestamp`, `Reservoirs` (air reservoir pressure in bar), `Motor_current` (compressor motor current), and `LPS` (low-pressure switch activated below ~7 bar), as well as `TP3` and other signals. The released file may be downsampled compared with earlier 1-Hz descriptions; **actual sampling intervals must be measured and reported from the original release, never assumed to be 1 Hz**.

**Known problem before any original CSV:** UCI's failure report table has four displayed failure episodes, but the 29 May–30 May 2020 row is followed by the phrase "Maintenance on 30Apr at 12:00", i.e. a repair **before** that particular failure. It is unsafe to silently correct 30Apr to 30May or attach it to an arbitrary other incident. The 18 April 2020 episode also lacks a clearly corresponding post-fault maintenance timestamp in the published excerpt. **Both are retained in the exclusion log**. We prospectively select **only two temporally consistent, explicitly dated maintenance anchors** and disclose that this is an *eligibility-selected sample*:
- Failure episode June 5 10:00–June 7 14:30; reported maintenance **2020-06-08 16:00**.
- Failure episode July 15 14:30–July 15 19:00; reported maintenance **2020-07-16 00:00**.

These are **publisher-reported maintenance dates**, not certified precise maintenance completion timestamps. The original telemetry's `timestamp` is naive wall-clock time; the UCI page does not specify a verified UTC offset. This study compares literal source/report clock labels, assumes—but **does not prove**—their temporal alignment, and makes **no exact UTC/cross-device synchronization claim**. The publisher's full source sensor recorder and failure log are separate sources of evidence but not independently timestamped physical instruments.

## File and original-source admission gates (fail closed)

Retrieve original bytes only from the exact official UCI URL. Do not use GitHub forks, a Kaggle substitute, Zenodo MetroPT (a different version), resampling, interpolation, a published anomaly feature or a synthetic reconstruction. Hash the exact original official ZIP as SHA256 and each exact archived CSV as SHA256 **on the first fetch**, store source URL, HTTP response metadata, bytes, ZIP member names, source SHA and first GitHub run ID. Source archive may have a separate documentation PDF; accept only one case-sensitive member with basename `MetroPT3(AirCompressor).csv` and reject duplicate names, traversal, symlinks, oversized decompressed files, >400MB ZIP size, >350MB CSV size or decompression ratio >1000. Never archive the large original data in this research repository; preserve a small outcome+source-hash manifest instead.

Require exact 1,516,948 data rows, no duplicate timestamp, strictly increasing source wall-clock timestamps (if not, mark invalid; don't sort), required `timestamp`, `Reservoirs`, `Motor_current`, `LPS` columns, all selected-window numeric values finite and nonnegative, LPS exactly 0 or 1. Never silently impute missing values. Record original first/last source stamp, exact number of rows, observed distribution of adjacent timestamp gaps (min/median/p95/max), and counts of zero/negative intervals. **The source version and its actual original sampling step, not the paper's different sample size, control the audit.**

The required fixed summary uses exactly **two windows per maintenance anchor**:
- Before: from `maintenance − 24h` inclusive to `maintenance − 2h` exclusive.
- After: from `maintenance + 2h` inclusive to `maintenance + 24h` exclusive.
The two-hour gap prevents interpreting an unknown repair duration or immediate transient as steady recovery. Exclude the 4-hour gap around maintenance from comparison without changing the original rows. Demand at least **30 original observations per window**, and at least 6 **distinct** one-hour buckets represented; otherwise the whole study is `RP_U009_SOURCE_OR_WINDOWS_INVALID`. Do not change the windows, anchors or minima after seeing the source.

For each of the **four fixed windows**, output these independent raw telemetry summaries (with no raw time-series dump):
- number of original source observations and covered distinct hourly buckets;
- `Reservoirs` median pressure in bar; fraction of valid source pressure readings **strictly below 7 bar**;
- `Motor_current` median amperes; fraction **above 5 amperes** as a secondary, operational-load proxy (not independently measured air delivery);
- `LPS` fraction active; discrepancy count between `LPS=1` and `Reservoirs<7` as a *diagnostic only*, since switch semantics/threshold hysteresis and sensor locations may differ; no assumption that they must match;
- optionally `TP3` if its column is present, but **it cannot replace** the registered pressure endpoint or alter eligibility.

All ratios use their exact eligible original observation denominator. No hidden filtering, no smoothing and no leakage from other time windows.

## Prespecified descriptive decision and negative controls

**Primary response statistic:** for each of the two retained maintenance events,
`delta_low_pressure = pre_fraction(Reservoirs < 7) − post_fraction(Reservoirs < 7)`.
Positive means *less low-pressure occupancy after* the publicly reported maintenance date; negative means worse, zero means no observed improvement. Full numerical change and pre/post absolute values must be reported for both, not just a favorable event.

The classification **`RP_U009_BOTH_REPAIRS_SHOW_LOWER_LOW_PRESSURE_OCCUPANCY`** requires both `delta_low_pressure > 0` (strict raw proportion comparison). Otherwise, with valid original source and both complete windows, report **`RP_U009_NO_TWO_EVENT_CONCORDANT_PRESSURE_IMPROVEMENT`**. Neither status alone establishes true restoration; no arbitrary 10-percentage-point threshold is introduced after seeing data.

Secondary operational-load and low-pressure-switch summaries must be disclosed even if they contradict the pressure change. No inference of improvement in actual delivered compressed-air workload or patient/biological functioning is permitted. Since the two reported maintenance events were selected for **chronological eligibility**, not randomly from all failures, **no population p-value, effect generalization, causality or original novelty** is claimed.

A **mandatory zero-signal negative control** evaluates a synthetic dataset with the same pre/post low-pressure distribution and must be classified as no concordant pressure improvement; a synthetic opposite-direction case must remain negative. Wrong chronology, duplicated source timestamps, missing LPS, inconsistent timestamps, corrupted ZIP, stale/changed source, oversized decompression, shortage of postrepair data, or a maintenance anchor before the reported fault end **invalidates** the original study rather than selecting new dates. A positive synthetic test is only assurance the source-free decision logic is reachable. All these synthetic/adversarial tests must execute **before any original ZIP download**.

Original source execution is only allowed by one dedicated GitHub Actions workflow, pinned to the first source runner commit, after full Python 3.11 and 3.12 regression runs. Preserve original failures, including inability to access the large official archive or chronological contradictions, as first-run results; do not rerun silently and declare a later result as first. Downloaded large source must be removed after deriving a small immutable audit manifest, avoiding filling a local user's drive. No commits or merges into main. Subsequent archival regression must verify the exact audit SHA and two-event outcome without re-fetching the 208 MB original.

## Literature, source quality and interpretation

- Davari et al. (2021), *Predictive maintenance based on anomaly detection using deep learning for air production unit in the railway industry*, DSAA 2021. Authors and operator had already analyzed these failures; we do not claim to discover them.
- Veloso, Ribeiro, Gama & Pereira (2022), *The MetroPT dataset for predictive maintenance*, Scientific Data 9:764, DOI https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-022-01877-3.
- Davari et al., official UCI MetroPT-3 Dataset 791, DOI https://doi.org/10.24432/C5VW3R.
- Martín-Fraile, Basurto, Sierra-García & Herrero (2026), *DAYPSCI: Event-based dataset for anomaly detection through fault injection in PLC-controlled industrial cyber-physical systems*, Data in Brief 68:113146, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2026.113146. DAYPSCI would be stronger as an explicit controlled fault-injection study with external labeling, but its university raw repository has not yet been directly retrieved or independently source-hashed in this session, so **no DAYPSCI results are claimed here**.
- USENIX CFDR data catalogue, https://www.usenix.org/cfdr-data: records some actual hardware-repair logs, but specifically COM1/COM2 are **not available for download** from the catalogue and do not have directly paired sensor/functional output.

Even a positive MetroPT-3 pressure comparison would be a **non-random, retrospective, two-event pre/post correlation with uncertain clock alignment**, not verified intervention causality. The new evidence level concerns physical measurements (pressure, motor current) and externally reported maintenance, whereas the earlier RP-U005/U006/U007 generated their own software service and RP-U008 studied a single network collector. No universal relational persistence law, model of self, cellular/oncological effect, emergent time, or transferable mechanism is established by this pilot.
