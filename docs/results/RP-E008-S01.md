# RP-E008-S01 — Independent discrete-event queue transfer

**Status: prospective, simulated observations NOT YET EXECUTED.**

This document was written before the full first-run simulator
was allowed to execute. Its existence and unit test success
do not imply an outcome.

## Research question

[The frozen RP-E008-S01 protocol](../preregistration/RP-E008-S01.md)
was committed to `main` at `2ca6d8e` before implementing
the transfer test. After the RP-E007 classical trend predictor
reduced MAE on a localhost FIFO service, we ask if the
same **unfitted** two-sample extrapolation beats a static
backlog prediction in a **different queue implementation**.

This is an **in-silico event model**, not a new physical
sensor, hosted live service, independent industrial dataset
or newly discovered stability theorem.

## Frozen first-run design

- 72 exact-integer-time FCFS queue scenarios: three
  irregular arrival modes × three workload-duration
  variants × eight seeded source replications.
- 36 diagnostic cases (replicate IDs 0–3) and 36
  prospective held-out cases (IDs 4–7). The
  predictors have no fitted parameters, and neither
  model is tuned to diagnostic or held-out outcomes.
- Two separately written exact event oracles:
  FCFS completion-time recurrence and independent
  chronological heap calendar with active-server FIFO.
  Both must produce **identical** completion times,
  25-ms sampled status, and recovery.
- Predictions at t=1000 ms use only q(925) and
  q(975), fixed B=1000/18 work/s from pretrial
  120-job simulated calibration, and the nominal
  factor-of-two postchange service rate.
- Primary Y is first **sampled** complete idleness
  at/after t=1000 with 500-ms confirmation. The
  exact end-of-service timestamp is archived
  separately; difference must be within [0,25) ms.
- Compute exact rational MAEs on the 36 held-out
  outcomes for static and two-sample trend. Declare
  PASS only if TREND is at least 10% better than
  STATIC, with STATIC MAE positive. Record NO_GAIN
  for a valid negative result; STOP if any source
  or second-engine invariant fails.

The new source files must retain every arrival timestamp,
job's variable duration, independent completion/event
traces, every sample, predictions, exact rational error
and reproducible source SHA-256. Replacing failed or
negative cases is forbidden.

## Interpretation before the outcome

A successful result would show **software model
robustness** of established queue-state estimation
in a second, independently authored mathematical
queue implementation, not transfer to real hardware.
A negative result would show that the fixed trend
extrapolation is not reliably useful across
these frozen software regimes.

Do not assert a measured physical outcome,
the predictive capacity of C=B/(I*tau), a newly
Lean-verified theorem or a causal theory from
this constructed simulator. Classical simulation
model verification and real-world validation
are distinct operations.
