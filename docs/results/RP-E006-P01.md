# RP-E006-P01 — First prospective held-out recovery-forecast test

**Status: NOT YET MEASURED.** This report exists before the
first RP-E006 experiment. No observed result, score or causal
effect is asserted here.

## Frozen design

[The immutable preregistration](../preregistration/RP-E006-P01.md)
was committed on `main` as `2aeabc6` before the new
instrumentation and fitted models were written.

A sequential training block (18 fresh-server interventions)
and a later hosted VM testing block (9 fresh-server
interventions) will each cover three loads (60, 100, 140
accepted units) and three configured action lags (15,
70, 120 ms), in locked independently shuffled orders.

At the forecast origin, **injector end**, only pre-end
measurements may enter the predictors. A shared continuous
monitor derives the recovery endpoint without using
candidate C or any competing prediction. The original
source logs, sampled status streams, actual service-applied
controller timestamps, and pre-end queue observations
must all be retained, hashed and reaudited.

## Models and immutable decision

The candidate is train-only ordinary least squares
`Yhat = max(0, alpha + beta/C)`. Primary comparators are:
training average and a fixed queue model
`(last_pre_end_queued + active)/(2B)`.

A passing result requires index-only **held-out MAE**
to improve on **both** comparators by at least 10%.
Otherwise the exact outcome is
`NO_REGISTERED_PREDICTIVE_GAIN`, except when invalid
measurement or model-identification inputs demand STOP.

Additional tau-only and load-only OLS fits provide
negative/diagnostic comparisons. Model coefficients are
frozen using the training data alone. The test-block
labels must never inform feature selection or training.

## Interpretation

The experiment would evaluate forecasting within one
configured FIFO software implementation; its cloud-hosted
VM observations do not establish a universal physics law
or an externally replicable stability metric.

A future independent service implementation and held-out
physical systems would be necessary before assigning the
hypothesis broader empirical support.

The backlog comparator is a finite-workload heuristic,
**not** the stationary Little's law `L=lambda*W`.
See [Little (1961)](https://doi.org/10.1287/opre.9.3.383).
